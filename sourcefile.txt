Bloody Week , Sunday21on sundayMay 28, 1871, refers to the bloodiest period and the final episode of the Paris Commune , during which the insurrection was crushed and its members executed en masse. It was part of the 1871 internal campaign led by the Versailles government against the established or planned insurrectional communes that were being experienced by several large French cities.
